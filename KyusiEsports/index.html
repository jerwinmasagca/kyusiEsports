ABOUT KYUSI ESPORTS

Kyusi Esports Community was established on November 2023 and was fully recognized and accredited on March 2024. Its general objective is to train, lead, and educate gamers inside Quezon City University and its satellite campuses about the Esports industry. That includes the following:

Tournaments

  • International
  • National
  • Regional
  • Municipalities
  • Barangays

Seminars

  • Introduction to Esports
  • Esports Industry Insights
  • Professional Gaming Etiquette
  • Career Pathways in Esports
